01 Mission

Health technology should work for every patient.

Vital Signals Systems is a biomedical technology company building sensors and AI software for every patient, across genders, ages, and communities. We design medical devices that capture the body's signals, and AI that interprets those signals with rigorous validation for the people it serves.

Our work centers the patients whose voices and bodies have too often been left out of how health technology is built, including women across the lifespan, people of color, and communities historically underrepresented in clinical research.

Better signals. Better care. For every body.
02 Our Approach

Hardware and AI, designed together from the start.

We build at two layers at once: the precision sensors that capture the body's signals, and the AI software that interprets them. Both are designed and validated for the people they serve, not retrofitted after the fact.

Sensors

Hardware that captures the body's signals

Multimodal biosignal sensors engineered for clinical reliability and real-world use. Built to capture physiological signals across the diverse bodies medical research has historically left out.

AI

AI that turns signals into care

Deep learning models that interpret biomedical signals and patient communication in context. Rigorously validated for the populations they serve, not just the populations they were trained on.

03 Focus Areas

Where our work matters most.

We concentrate on health domains where the gap between what technology can do and what people actually receive is widest.

F.01

Women's Health Across the Lifespan

Fertility, reproductive health, perimenopause, menopause, and chronic conditions that affect women throughout their lives, with attention to the experiences clinical research has historically overlooked.

F.02

Digital Health Equity

Sensors and AI tools designed and validated from the start to serve Black, Hispanic, and other communities medicine has too often missed, with rigorous testing across the populations they are built for.

F.03

Care That Listens

Connecting what our technology measures with how patients and clinicians understand and act on it, so the signal becomes care, not just data.
