01 / Mission

Building the next generation of human-centered health technology.

We are a biomedical technology company founded on a simple conviction: the future of healthcare depends on instruments and algorithms that work for everyone, not just the populations they were originally tested on.

Vital Signals Systems develops integrated hardware and software platforms for capturing, interpreting, and acting on biomedical signals. Our work brings together precision sensor engineering, machine learning, and rigorous communication science to create tools that are clinically credible, technically excellent, and built with marginalized populations in mind from day one.

We design sensors and AI for the people the health system was never built for, and prove they work for everyone else, too.
02 / What We Build

An integrated platform of hardware, software, and applied research.

Our portfolio spans three connected layers: the devices that measure physiology, the AI that interprets it, and the research that ensures it actually serves the people it's designed for.

Hardware

Multimodal Biosignal Sensors

Precision instruments for capturing the body's electrical, physiological, and movement signals. Engineered for clinical reliability and real-world durability.

  • EEG, ECG, and EMG signal acquisition
  • Wearable and ambient sensing modules
  • Integrated multimodal sensing platforms
  • Clinical-grade and research-grade form factors
Software

Health AI & Analytics

AI software that turns raw biosignals and patient language into clinically meaningful insight, validated for the populations it's deployed in.

  • Signal processing and digital biomarkers
  • Natural language analysis of patient communication
  • Decision-support dashboards for clinicians
  • Community-calibrated model validation
Applied Research

Funded Studies & Partnerships

Sponsored research with universities, foundations, and industry partners on women's health, reproductive health, and digital health equity.

  • SBIR / STTR and federal grant-funded R&D
  • Foundation-funded health communication studies
  • University and clinical site collaborations
  • Validation studies for health AI tools
Consulting

Strategy & Validation Services

We work with digital health teams, device makers, and labs to make sure their AI products actually work for the populations they're meant to serve.

  • Health AI auditing & bias evaluation
  • Community language calibration
  • Patient communication strategy
  • Mixed-methods study design
03 / Focus Areas

Where our work matters most.

We concentrate on health domains where the gap between what technology can do and what people actually receive is widest.

F.01

Women's Health Across the Lifespan

Fertility, reproductive health, perimenopause, menopause, and chronic conditions that have been historically underrepresented in clinical research and product design.

F.02

Digital Health Equity

Sensors and AI tools rigorously validated for Black, Hispanic, and other underserved populations, not retrofitted to them after launch.

F.03

Communication-Integrated Care

Bridging physiological data and patient-facing communication so that what devices measure actually translates into what patients understand and act on.
