01 Mission

Health technology should work for every patient.

Vital Signals Systems is a biomedical technology company building sensors and AI software for every patient, across genders, ages, and communities. We design medical devices that capture the body's signals, and AI that interprets those signals with rigorous validation for the people it serves.

Our work centers the patients whose voices and bodies have too often been left out of how health technology is built, including women across the lifespan, people of color, and communities historically underrepresented in clinical research.

Better signals. Better care. For every body.
02 Focus Areas

Where our work matters most.

We concentrate on health domains where the gap between what technology can do and what people actually receive is widest.

F.01

Women's Health Across the Lifespan

Fertility, reproductive health, perimenopause, menopause, and chronic conditions that affect women throughout their lives, with attention to the experiences clinical research has historically overlooked.

F.02

Digital Health Equity

Sensors and AI tools designed and validated from the start to serve Black, Hispanic, and other communities medicine has too often missed, with rigorous testing across the populations they are built for.

F.03

Care That Listens

Connecting what our technology measures with how patients and clinicians understand and act on it, so the signal becomes care, not just data.

03 Team

A founding team that bridges engineering and patient care.

Vital Signals Systems is built by founders who have spent their careers at the intersection of biomedical signals, artificial intelligence, and the human experience of health.

RC
Rim H. Chaif, Ph.D.
Co-Founder, Health AI Research

Dr. Rim Chaif holds a Ph.D. in Journalism and Mass Communications from the University of Kansas, with research at the intersection of health AI, natural language processing, and the experiences of patients navigating clinical systems. Her work examines how people communicate about their health online, how AI systems read or misread patient voices, and how health technology can be rigorously validated for the populations it serves.

She is the author of nine peer-reviewed publications spanning women's health, reproductive health, and online community dynamics, and the developer of an iterative human-AI validation framework for classification tasks in sensitive health contexts. She brings to Vital Signals Systems a foundation in investigative and humanitarian journalism across North Africa and Europe, and works in Arabic, French, English, and Italian.

AI
Ali K. Ibrahim, Ph.D.
Co-Founder, Biomedical Engineering

Dr. Ali Ibrahim is an electrical engineer and machine learning researcher at Florida Atlantic University, with a decade of published work in biomedical signal processing and deep learning across multiple body systems. His research spans epileptic seizure prediction from EEG, intracranial hemorrhage segmentation, diabetic retinopathy classification, breast cancer detection in medical imaging, and white blood cell identification for hematology, as well as applied AI for drug discovery and accessibility technologies.

At Vital Signals Systems, he leads the design of sensor architectures and the deep learning systems that turn the body's signals into clinical insight, drawing on multimodal AI architectures that work in real-world, noisy environments where conventional methods struggle.
